## Limits

- Static analysis of each project at one pinned commit, with the frozen scanner; no URL scanning, so black-box findings are out of scope.
- FastAPI projects have no route or code-pattern units: Penthera's route discovery reads JavaScript and TypeScript only.
- Dependency recall covers packages of at most 200 KB of source whose tarball or wheel could be read.
- Recall is an upper bound (labellers miss issues too) and covers only the categories listed.
