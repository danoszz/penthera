section: Changed

- The site follows the rules of The Elements of Typographic Style: real quotation marks and apostrophes on every page, a serif column of about 65 characters on reading pages, and small sticker labels in many finishes for every eyebrow and tag. The blog and its articles read like a teletext newspaper, always light on gray paper, with an interactive mosaic title, four colored page links, and the byline and an active section list in the margin.
